# Nagmani audio samples

Prepared by `tools/prep_snake_audio.mjs` from the "Desi Jungle" Strudel / Ableton starter kit
(`Desi Jungle - Ableton starter_1/ableton-starter`), whose `samples/` folder is the material the
Strudel sketch pulled from `github:tidalcycles/dirt-samples` (the `tabla` and `sitar` folders)
plus a General MIDI sitar note. Everything here is mono 16-bit WAV, resampled and trimmed.

| File | Origin | Use in the game |
|---|---|---|
| tabla_ke, tabla_na, tabla_tin, tabla_hi, tabla_ghe, tabla_ghe_short | `samples/tabla/36-41` | the Teentaal theka under the music; shed, lunge, banish, act and footfall effects |
| tabla_fill_a/b/c | `samples/tabla/extra/010, 013, 015` | fills at the end of a cycle |
| sitar_min_a/b/c | `samples/sitar-strums/41-43` (D minor) | strums brought down to C, the raga's Sa; the hollow ending |
| sitar_maj | `samples/sitar-strums/37` (D major) | the true ending |
| sitar_note | `samples/gm-reference/gm_sitar_root80_G#5` | the melody voice, pitched by playback rate |

this personal, non-commercial site and replace them before any commercial use.
